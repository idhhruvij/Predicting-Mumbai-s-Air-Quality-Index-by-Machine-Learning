**Predicting Mumbai’s Air Quality Index using Machine Learning**

📌 **Project Overview**

Air purity is critical to human health, with poor air quality being a leading cause of respiratory diseases, particularly in children. This project leverages Machine Learning to predict the Air Quality Index (AQI) for Mumbai, overcoming the limitations of traditional analytical methods by utilizing large datasets.
Our model achieves an approximate 93% accuracy in predicting AQI and monitors concentrations of key pollutants, including oxides of Carbon (CO), Nitrogen (NO), Sulfur (SO2), and Oxygen (O3). This tool serves as a predictive system for government bodies and citizens to take proactive measures against rising urban pollution caused by fossil fuel combustion and industrial activity.

🚀 **Key Features**

**High Accuracy:** Predicts Mumbai's AQI with ~93% precision.**

**Multi-Pollutant Analysis:** Forecasts levels of Carbon (CO), Nitrogen (NO), Sulfur (SO2), and Oxygen (O3)

**Big Data Integration:** Utilizes modern data analytics to process complex urban environmental variables.

**Health-Centric Insights:** Provides data necessary for preventive healthcare and environmental policy-making.

🛠️ **Tech Stack**

**Language:** Python

**Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn

**Model:** Simple Linear Regression

**Data Source:** https://airquality.cpcb.gov.in/ccr/#/caaqm-dashboard-all/caaqm-landing/aqi-repository

📊 **Dataset**

The project analyzes historical air quality data from Mumbai, focusing on:

**1. Industrial Variables:** Emissions from manufacturing zones.

**2. Traffic Patterns:** Impact of vehicular density on air quality.

**3. Chemical Concentrations:** Real-time tracking of harmful oxides.

📈 **Results**

The implementation of Big Data Analytics allows the model to identify patterns in pollutant accumulation that were previously difficult to track, providing a reliable early-warning system for the metropolitan region.
